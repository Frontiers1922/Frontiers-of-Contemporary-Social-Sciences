投稿指南Submission Guidelines

为确保投稿流程顺畅并保障期刊学术质量，请所有作者投稿前仔细阅读并遵守以下指南。投稿邮箱：ddshkxqy@126.com。To ensure a smooth submission process and to safeguard the journal's academic quality, all authors are advised to read and follow these guidelines before submitting. Submission email: ddshkxqy@126.com.

重要提示：本刊对优质稿件免收版面费与稿件处理费。Important: This journal is an open-access publication. It charges no article processing charges (APCs) and no fees of any kind for submission or publication.

稿件范围与类型Scope and Article Types

  • 原创研究论文：具有创新性的实证或理论研究成果Original research articles presenting innovative empirical or theoretical findings
  • 理论探新：提出新理论、新范式的探索性文章Theoretical explorations proposing new theories or paradigms
  • 学术综述：系统梳理领域研究进展的综述文章Review articles systematically surveying research progress in a field
  • 书评：高质量学术书评Book reviews of high scholarly quality
  • 研究简报：简短的前沿研究发现Research briefs reporting concise frontier findings

学术伦理声明Academic Ethics

稿件必须为原创作品，严禁抄袭、剽窃、一稿多投等学术不端行为。全部作者均对稿件内容承担学术责任，并确认全部署名。投稿时请声明不存在利益冲突。一经发现学术不端，将直接退稿并记录存档。Manuscripts must be original. Plagiarism, duplicate submission and other forms of academic misconduct are strictly prohibited. All authors share academic responsibility for the content and confirm the complete list of authorship. Any conflict of interest must be declared at submission. Confirmed misconduct will lead to immediate rejection and a recorded note in the journal's files.

格式要求Formatting Requirements

  • 语言：稿件以简体中文为主，中文稿件必须附带英文标题、英文摘要、英文关键词；亦可接收全英文稿件。Language: manuscripts are primarily in Simplified Chinese. Chinese-language submissions must include an English title, abstract and keywords. Manuscripts written entirely in English are also accepted.
  • 字数：研究论文5000字以上；书评3000字以上。Length: research articles of 5,000 characters or more; book reviews of 3,000 characters or more.
  • 文件格式：提交 Microsoft Word（.doc / .docx）稿件。File format: Microsoft Word (.doc / .docx).
  • 参考文献：遵循《信息与文献 参考文献著录规则》GB/T 7714-2015，保证引用完整、准确。References: follow GB/T 7714-2015 (Information and Documentation - Rules for Bibliographic References), with complete and accurate citations.
  • 匿名要求：为保障双向匿名审稿公正，稿件正文请隐去作者姓名、单位、基金项目、致谢等全部个人标识信息；作者信息、基金项目请写在邮件正文或单独投稿信中，不要放入论文正文。Anonymity: to ensure a fair double-blind review, remove all identifying information from the main text, including author names, affiliations, funding and acknowledgements. Author details and funding information should appear only in the cover email or in a separate cover letter, not in the manuscript body.

审稿流程Review Process

本刊实行双向匿名同行评议制度。投稿依次经过：编辑部初审、栏目主编分送、同行专家外审、主编终审。The journal adopts a double-blind peer review process. Submissions go through editorial screening, assignment by the section editor, external peer review by specialists, and a final decision by the editor-in-chief.

  • 初审周期：1-2周给出初审反馈。Initial screening: feedback within 1-2 weeks.

版权声明Copyright

稿件文责自负。本刊有权对来稿做文字、格式层面的必要修改与删节；若不同意修改，请投稿邮件中明确注明。Authors are solely responsible for the content of their manuscripts. The editorial office reserves the right to make necessary stylistic and formatting edits. Authors who do not agree to such edits should state this clearly in the submission email.

本刊属于开放获取期刊，稿件录用后，若无特殊书面说明，视为作者同意授权本刊进行纸本出版、网络电子版公开传播。作者保留文章署名权。The journal is open access. Upon acceptance, unless otherwise agreed in writing, authors authorize print publication and open online dissemination of the published version. Authors retain the right of attribution.

投稿方式How to Submit

请将实名和匿名稿件Word文档、投稿信息（作者姓名、单位、职称、联系方式、基金项目等）发送至邮箱：ddshkxqy@126.com，邮件主题格式建议：投稿-论文题目-作者。Please send the anonymized manuscript (Word) together with author information (names, affiliations, academic titles, contact details and funding) to ddshkxqy@126.com. Suggested subject line: Submission - Article Title - Author.

1. 期刊宗旨与定位1. Aims and Scope

《当代社会科学前沿》是在香港注册、实行同行评审的开放获取半年刊学术期刊。本刊立足当代社会现实，以“跨学科视野下社会科学的理论创新与实践回应”为理念，致力于面向全球学者、青年研究者搭建开放、平等、规范的国际化学术交流平台。本刊鼓励打破单一学科边界，推动社会学、政治学、马克思主义理论、哲学、教育学、心理学、公共政策等领域之间，以及人文社科与其他相关学科的对话交融，聚焦时代社会议题，产出严谨扎实、证据导向的原创研究成果。Frontiers of Contemporary Social Sciences is a peer-reviewed, open-access semiannual journal registered in Hong Kong. Grounded in contemporary social realities, the journal pursues "theoretical innovation and practical response in the social sciences from an interdisciplinary perspective," and is committed to building an open, equitable and rigorous international platform for scholarly exchange among researchers worldwide, in particular early-career scholars. It encourages work that crosses disciplinary boundaries, fosters dialogue among sociology, political science, Marxist theory, philosophy, education, psychology and public policy as well as between the humanities and other fields, and addresses pressing social issues through solid, evidence-based and original research.

2. 常设栏目2. Regular Sections

本刊栏目设置保持弹性，欢迎跨学科、跨栏目的综合性研究投稿，常设栏目包括：The section structure remains flexible, and interdisciplinary or cross-sectional submissions are welcome. The regular sections are:

  • 社会理论与马克思主义研究：马克思主义理论前沿、经典理论当代阐释、思想史梳理、社会本体与范式反思。Social Theory and Marxist Studies: frontiers of Marxist theory, contemporary interpretations of classical theory, history of thought, and reflections on social ontology and paradigms.
  • 政治与公共治理：国家治理、公共政策、比较政治、基层治理、制度变迁研究。Politics and Public Governance: state governance, public policy, comparative politics, grassroots governance and institutional change.
  • 教育与心理研究：教育创新模式、学习科学、认知与社会心理、青年心态、心理健康与社会适应。Education and Psychology: innovative educational models, learning sciences, cognitive and social psychology, youth mentalities, mental health and social adaptation.
  • 哲学、文化与思想研究：哲学基础理论、文化研究、跨文化比较、价值与伦理研究。Philosophy, Culture and Thought: foundations of philosophy, cultural studies, cross-cultural comparison, and values and ethics.
  • 数字社会与新质发展：数字社会、技术与社会、新质生产力、数字人文、社会转型研究。Digital Society and New-Quality Development: digital society, technology and society, new quality productive forces, digital humanities and social transformation.

3. 稿件类型3. Article Types

本刊接收原创研究论文、学术综述、理论探新、书评及高质量的研究简报。The journal publishes original research articles, review articles, theoretical explorations, book reviews and high-quality research briefs.

4. 办刊承诺4. Editorial Commitments

开放共享：坚持开放获取出版，保障学术成果无障碍传播；以学术质量作为唯一录用标准，面向海内外研究者提供公平的发表通道。Open access: We publish open access so that research can circulate without barriers, and we apply scholarly quality as the sole criterion of acceptance, offering a fair route to publication for researchers at home and abroad.

严谨求真：恪守学术规范，实行同行评议机制，倡导严谨、理性的学术争鸣，包容多元研究视角与理论范式。Rigour: We uphold academic norms and peer review, encourage rigorous and reasoned scholarly debate, and remain open to diverse research perspectives and theoretical paradigms.

扶持青年：打造对青年学者友好的学术共同体，提供建设性评审意见，助力青年研究者成长。Supporting early-career scholars: We build a community that is friendly to young researchers, provide constructive review reports, and help early-career scholars develop.

融通中外：致力于成为贯通不同学科、连接海内外社科研究者的学术桥梁，共同探索当代社会科学的发展方向。Bridging China and the world: We aim to serve as a bridge across disciplines and between Chinese and international social scientists, exploring together the future directions of contemporary social science.
